PhD Research Project · Universidad Complutense de Madrid

Bernardo's English Helper

An AI-powered English conversation app for adults 50+, built as part of my PhD research. Uses voice interaction, real-time feedback, and CEFR-aligned assessment to personalise each learner's experience 24/7.

  • Voice interaction with real-time AI responses
  • CEFR-aligned progress tracking across sessions
  • Customisable accent, pace, and topic selection
  • Currently in beta with Madrid-area research participants
Open App About the Project

How it works

  • Speech recognition

    The learner speaks; the app transcribes their words in real time using automatic speech-to-text.

  • Large language model

    An LLM acts as a conversational tutor - understanding the learner's input, correcting errors gently, and generating natural responses.

  • Text-to-speech

    Responses are read aloud in the learner's chosen accent, keeping the interaction fully spoken.

  • CEFR assessment layer

    Each session is mapped against CEFR can-do statements, tracking progress across sessions in a PostgreSQL database.
