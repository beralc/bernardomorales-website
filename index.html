Two products I lead, and what each one is for.

Grupo Edelvives · Product Manager · 2023 to present

ta-tum: from reading app to reading assessment

Goal. ta-tum started as a gamified reading tool. The aim was to turn it into a platform that gives teachers standards-aligned evidence of how their students read.

What I did.

  • Led the repositioning of ta-tum into a reading assessment platform aligned with PISA and PIRLS, with Universidad de Valencia.
  • Translated the international literacy frameworks (Procesos y Tareas) into product requirements.
  • Defined the assessment and reporting structure that gives teachers diagnostic reports on their students' reading skills.
  • Own the product roadmap and the budget, and work with an external development partner from spec to release.

Commercial and usage figures are confidential.

PhD research · Universidad Complutense de Madrid

English Helper: an AI voice tutor for adults over 50

Goal. Give adults over 50 a way to practise speaking English with an AI tutor, at any time, from their phone or computer.

How it works.

  • The learner speaks, and speech-to-text transcribes it in real time.
  • A large language model acts as a conversation partner and gives gentle corrections.
  • Text-to-speech answers in the accent and pace the learner chooses.
  • Each session is mapped to CEFR can-do statements, and progress is stored in PostgreSQL.

Status. In beta with research participants in a four-week study.
